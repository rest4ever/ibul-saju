Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

rest4 bible shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel to upload and schedule that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What data the tool uses

  • It requests permission to upload videos to the signed-in owner's YouTube channel and to read basic information about those uploads (for example, the video ID and upload status).
  • It does not read, collect or store any data about other people, viewers or other YouTube accounts.

How data is stored

  • The access token is stored privately by the channel owner and is used only to upload the owner's own videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
